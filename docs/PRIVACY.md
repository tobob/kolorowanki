# Privacy Policy — Kolorowanki

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Kolorowanki is a colouring app for young children, made by tobob ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles information.
Short version: **Kolorowanki does not collect, send or share any personal data. It works completely offline.**

**What we collect.** Nothing. Kolorowanki has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app makes no internet connections, and we never receive any information about you, your child or your device.

**What stays on the device.** To keep your child's colourings (which colour each part of each picture has) and the language chosen in the grown-ups' area, the app saves a small amount of data **only on your device**. We can't access it. "Start all pages again" in the grown-ups' area clears the colourings, and deleting the app deletes everything. If you use your device's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup), that copy is managed by Apple or Google under your account and their privacy policies.

**Photos.** "Save picture" adds a finished picture to the device's Photos with one tap, so a child can keep their own picture. The first time, the system asks once for permission to **add** to Photos. The app only adds pictures and never reads your photo library. Sharing a picture opens the system share sheet, which leads out of the app, so it is behind the parental gate; a shared picture goes only where you send it.

**Rating and feedback.** At the bottom of the grown-ups' area (behind the parental gate) there are two links that open outside the app: "Rate Kolorowanki" opens the App Store or Google Play, and "Send us feedback" opens a short feedback form on our website pastelki.club in your browser. The app itself sends nothing; the app version, build number, system (iOS or Android) and app language are only added to that link, so the form knows which app it is about. Whatever you choose to write in that form is handled by the website, under the privacy policy shown there. After a grown-up's sixth visit to that area, a small card may ask "Do you like Kolorowanki?" (at most twice, months apart); if you answer yes on an iPhone or iPad, the system's own rating prompt may appear (run by Apple). The visit count and your answer are remembered only on the device.

**Permissions.** Apart from adding pictures to Photos when a picture is saved, Kolorowanki doesn't ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location, and doesn't send notifications.

**Children.** Kolorowanki is designed for children aged 2–6. Because it collects no personal information, it doesn't need, and doesn't ask for, a child's or parent's personal details. The settings, this policy, the rating and feedback links and all information for adults are behind a parental gate. We follow the requirements of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR (including Article 8) and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code.

**Third parties.** We don't share data with anyone, because we don't have any. The app is downloaded through the App Store or Google Play; Apple and Google handle download information under their own privacy policies. The pictures come from the Pastelki library (https://pastelki.club) and are built into the app, so nothing is downloaded from there while your child plays.

**Your rights and deletion.** As we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. To remove the colourings stored on your device, use "Start all pages again" or delete the app.

**Changes.** If the app ever changes how it handles information, we'll update this page and the date above before releasing that version.

**Contact.** tobob — https://github.com/tobob/kolorowanki/issues
