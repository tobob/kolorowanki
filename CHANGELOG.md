# Changelog

All notable changes to this app. Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/);
versions are the store versions (`expo.version`). Store "What's New" texts per language live in
`store/whats-new.json`.

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- Share opens the share sheet on the first try after the grown-up check

## [1.0.3] - 2026-11-19

### Changed
- A few different calm tunes while colouring, and the music no longer starts over on the next picture
- Save picture saves straight to Photos; sharing is still for grown-ups
- Edyta has a new, warmer and calmer voice

### Fixed
- Share after the grown-up check now opens the share sheet, and the first tap on the answer always counts

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-19

### Added
- Tutorial: a new step where your child spreads two fingers to make the picture bigger, just like on every page

### Changed
- Calmer, slower music (soft piano and flute)
- New app icon and softer splash screen
- Edyta speaks more slowly, and in the tutorial the back and undo buttons sit exactly where they are on every page

### Fixed
- Edyta's voice: cleaner endings, no bleat in the last line

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-18

### Added
- Interactive spoken tutorial with Edyta the sheep: the child taps a colour, taps the apple, holds the leaf, colours the background and taps undo, while Edyta talks (synthetic voice, 17 languages) and praises each step.
- "Edyta's voice" switch in the grown-ups area.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added
- First release: a calm colouring book for children aged 2 to 6, with 48 pages on 4 levels made from Pastelki pictures.
- Tap to fill, hold for a ring of seven colours, an always-visible colour tray, pinch to zoom, undo and autosave.
- Saving a finished picture to Photos or sharing it, behind a parental gate.
- First-launch tutorial, music, 17 languages. Fully offline, no data collected.
