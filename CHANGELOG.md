## [Unreleased]

### Changed

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-18

### Added
- Interactive spoken tutorial with Edyta the sheep: the child taps a colour, taps the apple, holds the leaf, colours the background and taps undo, while Edyta talks (synthetic voice, 17 languages) and praises each step.
- "Edyta's voice" switch in the grown-ups area.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added
- First release: a calm colouring book for children aged 2 to 6, with 48 pages on 4 levels made from Pastelki pictures.
- Tap to fill, hold for a ring of seven colours, an always-visible colour tray, pinch to zoom, undo and autosave.
- Saving a finished picture to Photos or sharing it, behind a parental gate.
- First-launch tutorial, music, 17 languages. Fully offline, no data collected.
